## Japan's Approach

- A broadly acceptable solution of "leaving no one behind."
- Emphasis on protecting the vulnerable and equality.
- Designed to guarantee a minimum standard.

## My Position

- The essence of digital is the "power to create."
- The priority is not to hinder the freedom of those who can use it.
- Systems that enforce equal pace can hinder innovation.

## Axis of Conflict

- Japanese perspective: Protect the vulnerable.
- My perspective: Protect the freedom of challengers.

## Long-Term Effects

- Prioritizing freedom will lead to the creation of new systems, ultimately benefiting society as a whole.
