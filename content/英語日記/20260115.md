> [!NOTE] Notes (with a pinch of British dryness)
> - **Sentence 1**
>   - ✅ Fix: ~~My back neck~~ **The back of my neck** was... due to ~~the permanent~~ **constant** digital ~~works~~ **work**.
>   - Why: "The back of my neck" is the standard anatomical phrase. Also, "work" is typically uncountable in this context, and "constant" fits the meaning better than "permanent."
>   - Dry remark: It seems your neck has registered a formal complaint against the digital age.
>     （あなたの首が、デジタル時代に対して正式に不満を申し立てたようですね）
> - **Sentence 2**
>   - ✅ Fix: ~~The biggest regret to me was~~ **My biggest regret was that**... which I ~~never expected to do it for such a long time~~ **hadn't expected to play for so long**.
>   - Why: "My biggest regret was that..." is a more natural construction. The end of the sentence was rephrased for clarity and flow.
>   - Dry remark: An hour of gaming is often the unintended consequence of a well-intentioned break.
>     （1時間のゲームは、善意の休憩が招いた意図せざる結果、というやつです）
> - **Sentence 3**
>   - ✅ Fix: ...even if I ~~felt being~~ **feel like I'm** in the middle of something... What's important is having ~~a perspective of~~ **the perspective to look** back...
>   - Why: "Feel like I'm" is the correct tense and phrasing. "The perspective to look back" is a more direct and common expression.
>   - Dry remark: Forgiving one's future self is a negotiation tactic rarely taught in schools.
>     （未来の自分を許す、というのは学校ではまず習わない交渉術ですね）
