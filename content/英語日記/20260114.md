## 📝 Three Sentence Journal
 1. I'm so happy that I worked so hard today just for myself. I finished some of ~~tough~~ **the** tough tasks of publishing my past creations, that I had been previously wanting to do.
 2. Today I tried **a** new cafe ~~aside of~~ **from** the road **in** front of the station and it was kind of nice. I had **an** egg sand with salad and a cup of coffee, everything tasted quite good for its price.
 3. I tried to restudy and incorporate Krita, Blender and Stable diffusion into a single workflow to generate illustrations. What I'm aiming to do is ~~let my Macbook be more ideal with introducing~~ **make my Macbook more ideal by introducing** AI technology. 

> [!TIP] Key Sentences
> 1. key : description
> 2. ...

> [!NOTE] Notes (with a pinch of British dryness)
> - **Sentence 1**  
>   - ✅ Fix: some of ~~tough~~ **the** tough tasks  
>   - Why: You’re talking about a *known* set of tasks—the ones that have been quietly judging you for weeks.  
>   - Dry remark: Tasks you “meant to do” tend to develop remarkable longevity.  
>     （「そのうちやるつもり」のタスクは、なぜか異様に長生きする）
>
> - **Sentence 2**  
>   - ✅ Fix: ~~aside of~~ **from** the road **in** front of the station  
>   - Why: *aside of* doesn’t quite exist in polite English; *from* does the job, and *in front of the station* pins the location down sensibly.  
>   - Dry remark: A station café being “kind of nice” is already a minor triumph.  
>     （駅前のカフェが「まあまあ良い」だけでも、すでに小さな勝利）
>
> - **Sentence 2**  
>   - ✅ Fix: **an** egg sand  
>   - Note: In everyday English, **egg sandwich** is far more common. “Egg sand” sounds adventurous in ways you probably didn’t intend.  
>   - Dry remark: Sand is best kept at the beach, even when eggs are involved.  
>     （卵が絡んでも、砂はビーチに置いておくべき）
>
> - **Sentence 3**  
>   - ✅ Fix: ~~let my Macbook be more ideal with introducing~~ **make my Macbook more ideal by introducing** AI technology  
>   - Why: *make X … by ~ing* cleanly expresses cause and result; the original was understandable but rather heroic in its effort.  
>   - Dry remark: Your MacBook will appreciate the ambition and immediately ask for more RAM.  
>     （その意欲は評価されるが、MacBookは即座に「RAM足りない」と言い出す）
